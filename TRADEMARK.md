# Noam trademark policy

This policy covers the Noam name, wordmark, and logos. It does not grant a
license to the source code. See [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) for the current
code-licensing status.

## Referential use

You may refer to Noam truthfully in articles, reviews, compatibility statements,
and technical discussion. Do not imply sponsorship, affiliation, or endorsement.

## Uses that require permission

Written permission is required to:

- name a product, service, company, or fork "Noam" or a confusingly similar name;
- use the Noam logo or wordmark as another product's identity;
- offer a service under the Noam brand; or
- register related trademarks, domains, or social handles.

Permission requests should use the private contact method published on the
repository owner's GitHub profile.

This interim policy may change before the first public release.
